# Product stop clauses

At the product layer, commit the complete record and result marker before routing. Preserve
that marker while any failure route, selectable automatic event, or event routing remains;
only after those conditions finish may the stored verdict and evidence be reported and the
marker removed. A channel-unavailable product result is reported to the person and is not a
product observation or automatic event trigger.

Capability re-closure may narrow its changed scenario, but its folder regression signals
still rerun. Verification and closure use the fetched integration branch; platform-specific
signals use their declared remote evidence or their owning member without pretending that
an unrun signal passed.
