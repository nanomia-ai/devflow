# Product: Pantry Ledger

One household uses Pantry Ledger locally to record dated expenses in whole cents, organize them by
category, list and correct entries, and export a readable portable backup. Shared accounts, cloud
synchronization, credential issuance, and a graphical interface are outside the product.

Expenses and Categories are distinct business knowledge boundaries. The deletion consequence for a
category that still has expenses is not yet decided; the Categories Domain states the alternatives
and its confirmation condition. No other product question is open.
