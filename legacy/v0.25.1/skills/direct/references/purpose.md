# Purpose

Turn an accepted request into durable, user-approved units that Work can execute without losing request identity, duplicating planning, or letting planning perform execution.

Direct owns the direction boundary from planning-depth judgment through approved, Work-consumable cards. It consumes principles project-state's exact current origin and active scopes; determines which uncertainties need durable research and which work belongs in each independently executable unit; materializes one ordinary task or numbered research card per unit; and carries dependencies, order, parallelism, model tiers, and approval to Work.

Work performs research or implementation, Verify judges the result, and the external coordinator assigns and supervises agent processes. Direct does none of those jobs and never writes current K knowledge.

The reserved `00-project` root may contain many ordinary research-card branches. Each carries its exact Origin, begins pending, crosses the same proposal write-and-commit boundary, and routes to work only when canonical project-state reports effective approval for that exact card and origin.

Cards may list every affected knowledge owner, but direct never creates knowledge-landing markers. Later confirmed work or research synthesis owns those markers only after a durable conclusion has a long-lived consumer.
