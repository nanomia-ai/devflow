# Knowledge evidence policy

Knowledge paths prove address integrity only. For semantic sufficiency and provenance use [Provenance marks](knowledge/capsules-and-provenance.md#provenance-marks--unmarked-is-the-default); for trust and freshness use [Metadata and freshness](knowledge/opening-and-freshness.md#metadata-and-freshness); for writer and recovery authority use [Writers and replacement boundaries](knowledge/writers-and-migration.md#writers-and-replacement-boundaries). Do not infer truth, completeness, or ownership from a valid path alone.
