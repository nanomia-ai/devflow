# Ownership and re-entry

`.devflow/project/design.md` is the always-read current design owner; its same-stem design/K folder holds independently read design depth. A proposal remains in the Decision report until explicit approval; rejection changes no canonical design bytes, and approval writes the current document plus any changed design/K locus in the matching design commit.

The design stage may be entered for initial planning or a later whole-project, crosscut, or domain refinement. It selects from observed current state every time, rather than treating a previous pass as a permanent completion. A small project may keep the document compact when there is no independent design delta.

Design is not a knowledge-marker producer and never writes recursive capability knowledge. Later confirmed Work may emit the canonical knowledge-landing marker; it routes to Arch for managed capability/K. Adopt remains only as provenance for capability knowledge created during the initial unmanaged projection.
